INVESTMENT COMMITTEE MEMORANDUM — NORDHOLT LOGISTICS
Date: 14 March 2023
Prepared for the Investment Committee of Ashford Capital Partners Fund IV

1. Overview

Nordholt Logistics is a Nordic cold-chain logistics operator. At the date of
this memo the business runs 12 facilities and employs 640 people, principally
in Sweden. The company is strong in Sweden but remains under-represented across
the wider Nordic region.

2. Financial profile

Reported EBITDA margin is 18.6 per cent. Revenue is underpinned by multi-year
customer contracts, with an average contract length of approximately four
years. The management team is capable and has executed well against plan.

3. Growth plan

Management expects to complete two bolt-on acquisitions over the medium term.
Our base case envisages ARR growth to approximately EUR 95 million.
The Committee notes that projected returns are attractive but are not
guaranteed.
