Dear investor,


Halden Group is a limited partner in Kestrel Partners Fund IV. 
S1
This letter is about Harbourline Foods and the partial sale Kestrel has agreed with Northshore Consumer Partners.


S2
On 17 June 2026 Kestrel agreed to sell a 60 percent stake in Harbourline Foods to Northshore Consumer Partners at an enterprise value of GBP 420 million. 
S3
Kestrel will retain a 40 percent stake. 
S4
The sale is expected to complete in the third quarter.


S5
Harbourline produces chilled ready meals, pies and side dishes for grocery retailers in the United Kingdom and Ireland, from sites in Yorkshire and County Cork. 
S6
Kestrel invested in 2020, when Harbourline was a family owned business. 
S7
Since then the company has secured listings with five of the six largest UK grocers and the three largest Irish retailers, more than doubled its revenue, and doubled the capacity of its Cork site. 
S8
A newly automated production line in Yorkshire is now fully operational.


S9
In the quarter to 30 June 2026 Harbourline reported record volumes, launched a new range of premium ready meals, and secured a listing with a sixth major UK retailer. 
S10
Its valuation was marked up by 22 percent to the agreed transaction value.


S11
At 30 June 2026 the fund's net asset value stood at EUR 652 million. 
S12
The fund reports an IRR of 15.9 percent.
S13
 We will write again when Kestrel reports on the stake it holds.

Yours sincerely,

Halden Group
