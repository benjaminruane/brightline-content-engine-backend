MERIDIAN CAPITAL — FUND III ANNUAL REPORT
Date: 12 March 2020

Financial highlights for the year ended 31 December 2019.

Revenue for FY2019 was EUR 100 million, up from EUR 82 million the prior year.

At 31 December 2019 the fund held 12 portfolio companies across three sectors, and the underlying businesses employed 640 people in aggregate.
