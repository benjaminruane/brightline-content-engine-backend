COVERING NOTE

This report sets out the Fund's performance for the quarter ended 31 March 2025. Ostara European Mid-Market Fund II is a 2019 vintage European mid-market buyout fund. Figures below are as at the period end unless stated otherwise.

FUND POSITION

Total commitments stood at EUR 1.25 billion. Fund NAV was EUR 812 million. The Fund held 36 portfolio companies at quarter end, spanning business services, healthcare, and specialised industrials.

Net IRR since inception was 12.4%. Gross IRR since inception was 15.0%. TVPI was 1.51x and DPI was 0.43x. Returns are presented in euro, net of Fund-level fees and carry unless labelled gross.

CASH FLOWS IN THE QUARTER

Realised proceeds in the quarter were EUR 48 million, driven by the partial exit of Helvetic Components AG. Distributions to limited partners in the quarter were EUR 41 million. No new platform investment was completed in the period. Follow-on capital of EUR 9 million was deployed across two existing holdings.

PORTFOLIO COMMENTARY

Earnings across the portfolio were mixed. Healthcare holdings continued to contribute the largest share of NAV. Two industrial positions reported softer order books in the first weeks of the year, which the operating partners are addressing through cost and mix. No portfolio company was written down to nil in the quarter.

Helvetic Components remains a core holding after the partial exit. The remaining stake is carried at a valuation consistent with the transaction multiple on the sold slice, after a liquidity discount.

VALUATION

NAV of EUR 812 million was prepared by the manager and reviewed by the independent valuation agent. Unrealised investments are held at fair value in accordance with IPEV guidelines. Foreign-exchange translation into euro used period-end rates.

LOOKING AHEAD

The investment period has ended. The team is focused on supporting remaining holdings through the next phase of earnings growth and on orderly realisations where exit conditions are constructive. The next report will cover the quarter ending 30 June 2025.
