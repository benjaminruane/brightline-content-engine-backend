Meridian Capital completes acquisition of Lumen Specialty Chemicals

Frankfurt, Germany — 8 October 2025 — Meridian Capital ("Meridian"), the global private markets firm, today announced the successful completion of its acquisition of Lumen Specialty Chemicals GmbH ("Lumen" or "the Company"), a leading European producer of high-purity specialty chemicals for the electronics, pharmaceutical, and advanced materials industries. The transaction was first announced in June 2025.

Headquartered in Frankfurt, Lumen operates three production facilities across Germany and Belgium and employs approximately 850 people. The Company supplies more than 600 customers worldwide, with particular strength in ultra-high-purity solvents used in semiconductor wafer fabrication and active pharmaceutical ingredient manufacturing.

"Lumen represents exactly the type of investment opportunity Meridian is built to pursue," said Henrik Vossberg, Partner and Head of European Industrials at Meridian Capital. "It is a category-leading business serving structurally growing end markets, with a strong management team and meaningful opportunities for operational improvement and international expansion. We are delighted to partner with the Lumen team on the next phase of growth."

Lumen will be led by its existing Chief Executive Officer, Dr. Annika Brandt, who has held the role since 2019. The senior management team will remain in place. Meridian will support the Company's expansion strategy, with particular focus on the rapidly growing Asian electronics market and on selective acquisitions in adjacent specialty chemical categories.

"This is an important milestone for Lumen," said Dr. Annika Brandt, Chief Executive Officer of Lumen Specialty Chemicals. "Meridian brings deep expertise in our industry and a long-term perspective that aligns with our growth ambitions. Together we will accelerate our investment in capacity expansion, technology, and the talented people who make Lumen what it is."

The acquisition is the eighth investment from Meridian's flagship European Industrials strategy and brings the firm's total invested capital in the specialty chemicals sector to over EUR 2 billion across its global platforms.

Meridian was advised by Rothschild & Co (financial), Hengeler Mueller (legal), and Roland Berger (commercial). The seller was advised by Lazard.

About Meridian Capital
Meridian Capital is a global private markets firm with EUR 95 billion in assets under management. The firm invests in private equity, infrastructure, and private credit across Europe, North America, and Asia, on behalf of institutional and private wealth clients. Founded in 2002 and headquartered in Zurich, Meridian employs over 1,400 professionals across 14 offices worldwide.

About Lumen Specialty Chemicals
Lumen Specialty Chemicals is a leading European producer of high-purity specialty chemicals serving the electronics, pharmaceutical, and advanced materials industries. Founded in 1987 and headquartered in Frankfurt, Lumen operates three production facilities and supplies customers in over 40 countries.
