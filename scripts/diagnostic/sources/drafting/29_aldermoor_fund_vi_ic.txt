Subject:  Commitment to Aldermoor Capital Fund VI

1. RECOMMENDATION

The team recommends that the Committee approve a commitment of EUR 65 million to Aldermoor Capital Fund VI ("Fund VI"), a European mid-market buyout fund with a target size of EUR 1.1 billion and a hard cap of EUR 1.3 billion. First close is scheduled for January 2027, and the team proposes that Halden Group participates in the first close.

2. THE MANAGER

Aldermoor Capital ("Aldermoor") was founded in Munich in 2009 by four partners from a large-cap buyout firm and today has 38 investment professionals across Munich, Amsterdam and Stockholm. Aldermoor makes control investments in mid-market companies in the DACH region, the Benelux and the Nordics, with a sector focus on healthcare, business services and industrial technology. Its value creation model is operational, supported by an in-house portfolio team of nine specialists covering pricing, procurement, digital and finance function upgrades.

3. FUND VI STRATEGY

Fund VI will continue the strategy of its predecessors. Aldermoor expects to build a portfolio of 12 to 15 companies with enterprise values of EUR 150 million to EUR 500 million at entry, investing equity tickets of EUR 50 million to EUR 120 million. Target sectors are healthcare (in particular outsourced services and specialist products with recurring demand), business services and industrial technology, and the target geographies remain DACH, the Benelux and the Nordics. The fund will typically pursue majority positions alongside founders and management teams, with a meaningful share of deal flow sourced outside broad auctions.

4. INVESTMENT MERITS

Consistent, focused strategy. Aldermoor has applied the same sector and geographic focus across five funds, giving it deep networks in its niches and a strong position in founder-owned and family-owned businesses, which account for around 60% of its historical deal flow.

Proven operational model. The portfolio team has been built up since Fund III and has been a key driver of margin improvement across the portfolio, with average EBITDA margin expansion of 4.1 percentage points across realised Fund III and Fund IV investments.

Attractive market segment. The European lower mid-market offers a large opportunity set, less competition for assets than the large-cap segment and multiple exit routes, including sales to strategic buyers and larger sponsors.

Stable, aligned team. All four founding partners remain at the firm, and senior team turnover has been low. The general partner commitment to Fund VI will be 3% of fund size.

6. FIT WITH THE PRIMARY PROGRAMME

The Halden Group primary programme concentrates commitments on a core group of European mid-market managers with distinctive sourcing and operational capabilities, and re-commits where strategy and team are unchanged. Aldermoor has been a core relationship in the programme since 2015, and Fund VI keeps the programme's exposure to its preferred sectors in the European mid-market.

7. HISTORY WITH THE MANAGER

Halden Group has invested with Aldermoor since Fund III in 2015 and committed to Funds III, IV and V. We have also co-invested alongside Aldermoor on two occasions: in a Dutch laboratory services business alongside Fund IV in 2020 and in a Swedish industrial software company alongside Fund V in 2023. Both co-investments are performing in line with plan. We sit on the limited partner advisory committees of Funds IV and V.

8. KEY TERMS

Management fee and carried interest are in line with Fund V and market standard for the segment. The investment period is five years and the fund term ten years, with two one-year extensions.

9. KEY RISKS

Fund V is still early in its life and its performance cannot yet be assessed.
Fund VI is larger than its predecessors; the team will need to maintain deal selectivity as average ticket sizes increase.
Exit environment: mid-market exit volumes in Europe have been below their long-term average since 2023.

10. CONCLUSION

The investment team considers Fund VI an attractive opportunity to continue a long-standing relationship with a focused European mid-market manager whose operational model has delivered first-quartile results in its two most recent assessable funds, investing in sectors and geographies where Aldermoor has a clear sourcing advantage.
