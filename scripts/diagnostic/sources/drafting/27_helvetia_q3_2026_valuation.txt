HALDEN GROUP
VALUATION RECOMMENDATION - STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL

To:       Valuation Committee
From:     European Industrials Team
Date:     14 October 2026
Subject:  Helvetia Precision Components AG - valuation as at 30 September 2026

1. RECOMMENDATION

The team recommends increasing the fair value of the Halden Group investment in Helvetia Precision Components AG ("Helvetia") by 14.0% compared with the 30 June 2026 valuation. The valuation methodology is unchanged.

2. METHODOLOGY

Helvetia is valued on an enterprise value to last-twelve-months EBITDA basis, using the trading multiples of the established listed peer set (eight precision components manufacturers serving medical, aerospace and semiconductor end markets), adjusted for size and growth in line with prior quarters. Equity value is derived after deducting net debt at the valuation date. The CHF-denominated equity value is translated into the fund's reporting currency at the quarter-end rate.

3. VALUATION BRIDGE (30 JUNE 2026 TO 30 SEPTEMBER 2026)

Driver                                   Change in fair value
LTM EBITDA growth                        +4.6%
Peer multiple movement                   +6.1%
Net debt                                 +0.0%
Foreign exchange (CHF strength)          +3.3%
Total                                    +14.0%

LTM EBITDA rose 3.8% over the quarter, contributing approximately one third of the uplift. The applied multiple increased from 11.2x to 11.9x, reflecting a re-rating of the listed peer group following strong results across the medical technology supply chain. The Swiss franc strengthened against the euro over the period.

4. TRADING UPDATE

Medical (54% of revenue): demand remained solid. Orthopaedic customers maintained first-half order patterns and the largest medical account continued to ramp volumes on a knee system launched in 2025.

Aerospace (29% of revenue): volumes were stable, with build-rate increases at the two main customers feeding through as planned.

Semiconductor (17% of revenue): softer. Two equipment customers deferred tool orders into 2027, and segment revenue was below both the prior quarter and budget.

Group EBITDA margin improved by 0.6 percentage points over the quarter, helped by a higher share of medical work in the mix. Procurement savings remain on track against the year-one plan and contributed modestly. Net debt was broadly flat, with cash generation offset by a seasonal working capital build.

5. VALUE CREATION PLAN

The original investment case rested on a defensive medical and aerospace franchise with a smaller cyclical semiconductor exposure, and a plan to expand margins through footprint consolidation, procurement and automation. Footprint consolidation at Winterthur is in planning, procurement is ahead of schedule, and the first automation cells at the Brno plant were commissioned in August. The add-on acquisition of Morava Precision is being prepared and is not reflected in this valuation.

6. OUTLOOK AND SENSITIVITIES

The semiconductor segment is expected to remain soft into the first half of 2027. If equipment orders do not recover, LTM EBITDA growth is likely to slow, and a de-rating of the peer group would reverse part of this quarter's uplift. A 0.5x change in the applied multiple would move fair value by approximately 5%. A 5% weakening of the Swiss franc against the euro would reduce fair value by approximately 5%.

7. CONCLUSION

The uplift this quarter is driven predominantly by market factors, with peer multiples and currency together accounting for around two thirds of the increase, while underlying trading in medical and aerospace remains in line with the investment case.
