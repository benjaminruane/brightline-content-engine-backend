PRESS RELEASE

Kestrel Partners to acquire majority stake in Fjordline Software

Oslo / London, 18 September 2026 - Kestrel Partners, the European software and technology-enabled services investor, today announced that Kestrel Partners Fund IV has agreed to acquire a majority stake in Fjordline Software AS ("Fjordline"), a leading provider of planning software for freight and logistics operators. Founder and Chief Executive Ingrid Saether and the senior management team will reinvest alongside Kestrel and retain a significant minority stake. Financial terms were not disclosed.

Founded in Oslo in 2011, Fjordline develops cloud-based software that helps freight operators plan truck and vessel loads, manage container yards and allocate delivery slots. Its platform is used daily by more than 300 logistics companies across Norway, Sweden, Denmark and northern Germany, and handles over 40 million planned loads each year. The company employs around 140 people and has grown recurring revenue by more than 20% per year over the past three years, driven by customers extending the platform across their networks and by new operator wins.

Ingrid Saether, Chief Executive of Fjordline, said: "Our customers run their operations on Fjordline every day. Kestrel understands software that sits at the heart of physical supply chains, and their support will help us expand into new markets and accelerate product development, including our new dock scheduling module."

Tom Ashworth, Partner at Kestrel Partners, said: "Fjordline is exactly the type of business we look for: mission-critical software, deeply embedded in customers' workflows, with a founder-led team and a long runway for growth. We are delighted to be backing Ingrid and the team for the next phase of their journey."

Kestrel intends to support Fjordline's expansion into the Benelux and Poland and its pipeline of product launches, as well as selective acquisitions of complementary planning tools.

Completion is expected in early October 2026, subject to customary conditions.

About Kestrel Partners
Kestrel Partners invests in European software and technology-enabled services businesses with enterprise values between EUR 100 million and EUR 600 million. Founded in 2008, Kestrel has offices in London, Amsterdam and Stockholm and manages over EUR 3 billion across four funds.
