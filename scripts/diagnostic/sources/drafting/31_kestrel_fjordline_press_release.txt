FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Oslo, 2 October 2026

Kestrel Partners acquires Fjordline Software

Kestrel Partners today announced that Kestrel Partners Fund IV has agreed to acquire Fjordline Software, an Oslo-based company whose software helps freight operators plan loads, yards, and delivery slots. The company was founded in 2013. It employs about 240 people, most of them in Oslo, with smaller teams in Gothenburg and Hamburg.

Fjordline's customers are logistics companies. They use the software every day to decide what goes on a truck or a vessel, which yard bay a container uses, and which delivery window a customer has been promised. The product is sold on a subscription. The customer list includes regional freight operators across Norway, Sweden, Denmark, and northern Germany. Management reports that the number of loads planned on the platform has grown at a double-digit annual rate over the past three years, as existing customers have put more of their network onto the system and as a smaller number of new operators have joined.

The company is led by its founder, Ingrid Solberg, who will continue as chief executive. The senior product and customer teams stay with the business.

Ingrid Solberg said: "Freight operators already run a tight network. What they ask us for is a plan that survives a late ship and a full yard. Kestrel has spent the time to understand that job, and we are glad to build the next version of the product with them."

Kestrel's rationale is that Fjordline is already embedded in the daily work of its customers, in a part of logistics where a missed slot is expensive and a trusted system is not swapped out lightly. Fund IV looks for software businesses of that kind: specialist, in daily use, and able to grow with the customers they already have. Fjordline is that business in Nordic and north-German freight.

Marcus Hale, partner at Kestrel Partners, said: "Fjordline does a specific job for freight operators, and those operators already run their morning on it. That is the business we wanted to back."

The transaction is expected to complete in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to customary conditions. Terms of the consideration were not disclosed.

About Kestrel Partners
Kestrel Partners is a private investment firm. Fund IV invests in software and services companies in Europe. This announcement is for information. It is not an offer to sell interests in any fund.
