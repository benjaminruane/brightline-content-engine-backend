PORTFOLIO DEVELOPMENTS

Harbourline Foods
Harbourline had an outstanding quarter. Volumes reached a record level following the launch of a new range of premium ready meals with two national grocers, and the company secured a listing with a sixth major UK retailer. The newly automated production line in Yorkshire is now fully operational. On 17 June we agreed the sale of a 60% stake to Northshore Consumer Partners, valuing Harbourline at an enterprise value of GBP 420 million; completion is expected in the third quarter, subject to regulatory approvals. We have revalued Harbourline to the agreed transaction value, an increase of 22% in the quarter, and it was the main contributor to the Fund's NAV growth.

Aurelia Health
Aurelia continued to roll out its rostering platform at existing hospital group customers and signed one new customer in the Netherlands. The valuation was held broadly flat. Management is assessing several small add-on opportunities in the Nordic region.

Brightwater Labs
Brightwater traded in line with the first quarter. The contract with its largest customer, a national testing network, comes up for renewal in the third quarter, and management is in discussions on terms. Brightwater's valuation was unchanged.

Other holdings
Corvane Data, Plinth Systems and Tallowmere were valued broadly unchanged. Tallowmere completed a small add-on acquisition in workforce analytics.
