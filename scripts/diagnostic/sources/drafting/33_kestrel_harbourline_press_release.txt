PRESS RELEASE

Kestrel Partners agrees sale of majority stake in Harbourline Foods to Northshore Consumer Partners

London, 17 June 2026 - Kestrel Partners today announced that Kestrel Partners Fund IV has agreed to sell a 60% stake in Harbourline Foods Limited ("Harbourline"), the UK and Irish chilled prepared foods producer, to Northshore Consumer Partners. The transaction values Harbourline at an enterprise value of GBP 420 million. Kestrel Partners Fund IV will retain a 40% stake and continue to support the business alongside Northshore and the management team.

Harbourline produces chilled ready meals, pies and side dishes for major grocery retailers in the United Kingdom and Ireland from sites in Yorkshire and County Cork. When Kestrel invested in 2020, Harbourline was a family-owned business supplying a handful of regional retailers. Under Kestrel's ownership the company has secured national listings with five of the six largest UK grocers and the three largest Irish retailers, more than doubled its revenue, doubled the capacity of its Cork site and invested in automated production lines that have lifted on-time, in-full delivery rates to over 98%.

Claire Dunmore, Chief Executive of Harbourline, said: "Kestrel backed us to transform from a regional supplier into a national partner to the UK and Irish grocers. We are excited to welcome Northshore, whose experience in branded and private-label food will support our next stage of growth."

James Kerr, Managing Partner at Kestrel Partners, said: "Harbourline is a great example of our approach of backing strong management teams to professionalise and scale. We are proud of what the team has achieved and are pleased to remain a significant shareholder as Harbourline enters its next chapter with Northshore."

The transaction is expected to complete in the third quarter of 2026, subject to customary regulatory approvals.

About Kestrel Partners
Kestrel Partners invests in European software and technology-enabled services businesses, as well as selected consumer and industrial companies, with enterprise values between EUR 100 million and EUR 600 million. Founded in 2008, Kestrel has offices in London, Amsterdam and Stockholm and manages over EUR 3 billion across four funds.
