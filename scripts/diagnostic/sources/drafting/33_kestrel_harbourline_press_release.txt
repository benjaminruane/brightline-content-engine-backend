London, 17 June 2026

Kestrel Partners agrees to sell a majority stake in Harbourline Foods

Kestrel Partners announced today that Kestrel Partners Fund IV has agreed to sell a 60 percent stake in Harbourline Foods to Northshore Consumer Partners. The fund will retain a 40 percent stake. The agreement was signed on 17 June 2026. Completion is expected in the third quarter of 2026, subject to customary conditions.

The transaction values Harbourline Foods at an enterprise value of EUR 910 million.

Harbourline Foods produces chilled foods for retailers in the United Kingdom and Ireland. The company supplies prepared meals, pies, and side dishes from plants in Yorkshire and County Cork. It was a family business when the fund invested, selling mainly into a small group of regional retailers. Over the holding period the customer list widened to national retailers in both markets, and the Cork plant was expanded to serve the Irish listings. The product range stayed in chilled food. The company did not move into frozen food or into foodservice. Retailers order against a weekly plan, and the plants have spent the last two years raising the share of orders they deliver in full and on the day they were promised. That service record is the operating result the announcement is able to point to, alongside the wider customer list.

The chief executive, Helen Ward, who joined during the holding period, will stay with the company. Northshore Consumer Partners will appoint directors in line with the 60 percent stake. Kestrel will keep directors in line with the 40 percent it retains.

Helen Ward said: "Retailers want a reliable chilled range and a plant that can take a promotion without missing a delivery. That has been the work. We are glad Northshore wants to continue it, and glad Kestrel is staying for the next stage."

Marcus Hale, partner at Kestrel Partners, said: "Harbourline became a supplier the national retailers could plan around. Selling a 60 percent stake brings in a partner for the next investments, and we are keeping 40 percent because we want to stay with the company."

Northshore Consumer Partners is a private investment firm focused on consumer businesses. It has not previously owned Harbourline.

About Kestrel Partners
Kestrel Partners is a private investment firm. Fund IV invests in software and services companies and, in this case, has owned a food manufacturer. This announcement is for information. It is not an offer to sell interests in any fund.
