KESTREL PARTNERS FUND IV
CAPITAL CALL NOTICE
This notice is addressed only to limited partners of Kestrel Partners Fund IV. It is not for further distribution.

To:        Halden Group, as limited partner
From:      Kestrel Partners, investor services
Date:      6 October 2026
Subject:   Drawdown notice, Fund IV

Halden Group is a limited partner in Kestrel Partners Fund IV. This notice calls EUR 3.75 million, which is 9.4 percent of Halden's commitment of EUR 40 million. The amount is due on 28 October 2026, in euros, to the fund account named in the original side letter.

Of the EUR 3.75 million, EUR 3.52 million funds investments and EUR 0.23 million is the management fee for the quarter. The fee is stated here so the limited partner can reconcile the notice. It is part of this call.

The investment portion funds two uses.

The first is the acquisition of Fjordline Software, a Norwegian company that sells software used by freight operators to plan loads, yards, and delivery slots. Fjordline's customers are logistics companies in the Nordic region and in northern Germany. Kestrel has signed the purchase. The fund's share of the equity, being the portion this call funds for that deal, is included in the EUR 3.52 million above. Fjordline fits Fund IV's strategy of buying software businesses that sit inside a physical supply chain and are already used daily by their customers.

The second is a follow-on investment in Aurelia Health, a company the fund already owns. Aurelia provides software to hospital groups for rostering clinical staff. The follow-on funds a bolt-on acquisition by Aurelia of a smaller rostering business in Denmark. The follow-on keeps Aurelia as the owner of the combined product and is the sort of add-on the original Aurelia paper described. The amount of the follow-on attributable to Halden is included in the investment portion of this call, not stated again as a separate draw.

After this notice is paid, Halden's called commitment will stand at 62 percent of the EUR 40 million commitment. Unfunded commitment remains. No other draw is noticed in this letter.

Please pay EUR 3.75 million by 28 October 2026. Late payment follows the fund agreement. Questions can be sent to the investor-services address on the fund's reports.

Kestrel Partners
6 October 2026
