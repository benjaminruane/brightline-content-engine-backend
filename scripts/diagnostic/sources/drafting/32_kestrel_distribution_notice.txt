KESTREL PARTNERS FUND IV
DISTRIBUTION NOTICE
This notice is addressed only to limited partners of Kestrel Partners Fund IV. It is not for further distribution.

To:        Halden Group, as limited partner
From:      Kestrel Partners, investor services
Date:      8 October 2026
Subject:   Distribution following the Harbourline Foods sale

Halden Group is a limited partner in Kestrel Partners Fund IV. This notice distributes proceeds from the sale of a stake in Harbourline Foods.

The sale completed on 30 September 2026. The fund sold a 60 percent stake and retains 40 percent. The gross multiple on the stake sold is 2.8 times. That multiple is on the stake sold. It is not a multiple on the whole company, and it is not a net figure after the fund's charges.

The distribution to Halden is EUR 22.4 million, to be paid on 20 October 2026. Of that amount, EUR 7.8 million is a return of capital and EUR 14.6 million is gain. A recallable portion of EUR 2.0 million may be drawn again under the fund agreement. The recallable portion is included so the limited partner can record it. It is not a statement that a further draw has been noticed.

The proceeds came from the Harbourline sale and from no other exit. Harbourline Foods produces chilled foods for retailers in the United Kingdom and Ireland. The fund has held the company for several years. The buyer of the 60 percent stake, and the story of the company, were set out in the manager's announcement of 17 June 2026, when the agreement was signed. This notice is the cash event that follows completion on 30 September 2026.

Please confirm bank details if they have changed since the last distribution. The payment date is 20 October 2026. No action is required to receive the funds if the details on file are current.
