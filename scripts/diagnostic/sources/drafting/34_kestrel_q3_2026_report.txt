PORTFOLIO DEVELOPMENTS

Brightwater Labs
Brightwater, which provides analytical software and laboratory services to water testing and food safety networks, lost its largest customer during the quarter. The customer, a national testing network that accounted for around 35% of Brightwater's revenue, terminated its contract after being acquired by a group that operates its own laboratories. The loss was not related to service quality, and Brightwater's other contracts have renewed as planned. Management is reducing the cost base in line with lower revenue and is in advanced discussions with two mid-sized networks, although neither has yet signed. We have reduced the valuation of Brightwater by 41% to reflect the lower earnings base and the time needed to rebuild it. Brightwater was the main driver of the quarter's NAV decline.

Aurelia Health
Aurelia, our clinical staff rostering software business, had a strong quarter. Two existing hospital group customers extended the platform to additional sites, and annual recurring revenue grew 9% in the quarter. In September Aurelia agreed to acquire Medivakt ApS, a Danish rostering provider, which will give the company a foothold in the Danish hospital market; the acquisition was funded through a follow-on investment drawn in Capital Call No. 11. We increased the valuation of Aurelia by 18%, which offset part of the Brightwater write-down.

Harbourline Foods
On 30 September we completed the sale of a 60% stake in Harbourline to Northshore Consumer Partners, following the agreement announced on 17 June. The sale generated a gross multiple of 2.8x on the cost of the shares sold. The Fund retains a 40% stake, which is held at the transaction value.

Fjordline Software (subsequent event)
On 18 September the Fund signed an agreement to acquire a majority stake in Fjordline Software, an Oslo-based provider of planning software for freight and logistics operators. The acquisition completed on 2 October, after the end of the period, and Fjordline will be included in the portfolio from the fourth quarter.

Other holdings
Our three smaller software holdings, Corvane Data, Plinth Systems and Tallowmere, were valued broadly unchanged. Corvane renewed its largest customer contract for five years; Plinth delayed a product release to the first quarter of 2027.

PIPELINE

We are in exclusive negotiations to acquire Selkirk Analytics, a provider of data integration services for mid-sized manufacturers, and expect to reach a decision in the fourth quarter.

OUTLOOK

With Fjordline now in the portfolio and Fund IV 62% called, we expect to make one or two further platform investments before the end of the investment period. Our priorities for the coming quarters are rebuilding Brightwater's customer base, integrating Medivakt into Aurelia and supporting Fjordline's international expansion. We remain confident in the overall quality of the portfolio.
