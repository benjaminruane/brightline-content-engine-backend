Subject:  Project Vale - acquisition of five LP interests from Vestmark Pension Scheme

1. RECOMMENDATION

The team recommends that the Committee approve the acquisition of limited partnership interests in five buyout funds from Vestmark Pension Scheme ("Vestmark"), at a price of 88% of net asset value as reported by the general partners at 30 June 2026. Closing is targeted for November 2026, subject to general partner consents.

2. TRANSACTION BACKGROUND

Vestmark is consolidating its private equity programme around a smaller number of manager relationships and approached a limited number of secondary buyers with a defined list of interests. Halden Group was invited into the process on the strength of its existing relationships with two of the four managers. The transaction was negotiated bilaterally following a short second round.

3. THE PORTFOLIO

Fund                        Manager             Vintage   Region          Status
Northlane Buyout III        Northlane Capital   2016      Europe          Harvesting
Cinderwell Partners II      Cinderwell          2017      North America   Harvesting
Hartham Equity IV           Hartham             2018      Europe          Harvesting
Northlane Buyout IV         Northlane Capital   2019      Europe          Harvesting
Pellam Mid-Market I         Pellam              2021      Europe          Investing

The interests give exposure to 47 underlying companies, predominantly in the European and North American mid-market, across business services (34% of NAV), industrial technology (28%), healthcare (24%) and consumer (14%, almost entirely within Cinderwell Partners II). Four of the funds are past their investment periods and are realising assets; Pellam Mid-Market I is 55% called and continues to make new investments. Weighted by NAV, the portfolio is 6.1 years old.

4. INVESTMENT MERITS

Attractive entry price. The purchase price represents a 12% discount to reported NAV. Based on our bottom-up review of the 25 largest companies, representing 81% of NAV, we consider the reported values to be well supported, with valuation multiples in line with listed and transaction comparables.

Near-term liquidity. The 2016 to 2019 funds hold companies that are largely through their value creation plans. General partner exit projections and our own assessment point to a significant share of NAV being realised over the next three years.

Balanced maturity. The inclusion of Pellam Mid-Market I, which is still investing, adds exposure to newer assets and provides a source of continued value creation once the older funds are fully realised.

Diversification. The portfolio spans four managers, five vintages and 47 companies, with no single company above 6% of NAV.

5. FIT WITH THE SECONDARIES PROGRAMME

The Halden Group secondaries programme focuses on acquiring LP interests in mid-market buyout funds managed by GPs we know well, where our information advantage allows us to underwrite assets with confidence and act quickly in negotiated processes. Project Vale is a typical example of a seller-led portfolio sale in this segment.

6. HISTORY WITH THE MANAGERS AND ASSETS

Halden Group is an existing investor in Northlane Buyout III, Northlane Buyout IV and Cinderwell Partners II, and has held these positions since the funds' first closings. We have reviewed quarterly reporting on these funds throughout their lives and know most of their portfolio companies well. We undertook full due diligence on Hartham Equity IV in connection with a separate secondary transaction in 2022, which did not proceed on price grounds. Pellam Mid-Market I is a new manager relationship; our diligence comprised a review of the fund's last four quarterly reports, the underlying company valuations and a call with the general partner.

7. TERMS

The price of 88% of 30 June 2026 NAV will be adjusted for cash flows between the reference date and closing. Unfunded commitments transfer to Halden Group with the interests and are concentrated in Pellam Mid-Market I. Consents have been requested from all four general partners; none has been declined.

8. KEY RISKS

Valuation: the discount assumes reported values are broadly achievable at exit. Two companies in the older funds have been held longer than planned.
Consents: a refusal on any fund would require a decision on whether to proceed with the remainder.
Blind-pool exposure: Pellam Mid-Market I can still make new investments, and our knowledge of this manager is less deep than for the others.
Manager concentration: two of the five interests are managed by Northlane Capital.

9. CONCLUSION

The investment team considers Project Vale an attractive opportunity to acquire a diversified mid-market portfolio, largely managed by GPs we know well, at a meaningful discount to well-supported reported values, with visible near-term liquidity from the older funds complemented by continued growth exposure through Pellam Mid-Market I.
