FUND OVERVIEW

Ostara European Mid-Market Fund II ("Ostara II" or "the Fund") is a 2019 vintage buyout fund investing in control and significant-minority positions in European mid-market companies. The strategy concentrates on business services, healthcare, and specialised industrials, typically with enterprise values between EUR 75 million and EUR 400 million at entry.

The Fund is advised by Ostara Capital Partners. The investment team is led by Managing Partner Clara Voss and Partner Henrik Dahl.

PORTFOLIO

The Fund held 34 portfolio companies as at 31 March 2025. Sector mix by NAV is approximately 38% business services, 31% healthcare, and 31% specialised industrials. The five largest positions together represent 42% of NAV.

Remaining value is concentrated in companies that have completed the first phase of operational work and are now compounding earnings.

GOVERNANCE AND REPORTING

The Fund reports to the Limited Partner Advisory Committee each quarter. Valuation is prepared by the manager and reviewed by an independent valuation agent. The next LPAC meeting is scheduled for May 2025.
