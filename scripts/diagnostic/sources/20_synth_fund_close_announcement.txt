FUND CLOSE ANNOUNCEMENT — STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL

To:        All Meridian Capital personnel
From:      Michael Vossberg, Chief Executive Officer
Date:      11 December 2025
Subject:   Meridian European Industrials Fund V — Final Close at Hard Cap

I am delighted to announce that Meridian European Industrials Fund V ("Fund V") has held its final close at hard cap, with total commitments of EUR 4.8 billion. This represents the largest fund in Meridian's history and a meaningful step-up from Fund IV, which closed at EUR 3.2 billion in 2021.

The fundraise has been completed in just eleven months from first close, well ahead of our original eighteen-month target. Demand exceeded the hard cap by approximately EUR 1.2 billion, and we have been disciplined in maintaining the cap to preserve fund discipline and avoid scaling beyond our deployment capacity.

The LP base reflects the strength and global character of Meridian's franchise. Fund V received commitments from 92 institutional investors across 26 countries. Re-up rates from existing LPs were 96% by capital. New LPs include three of the largest US public pension funds, two sovereign wealth funds in Asia, and a number of family offices and endowments in Europe and North America.

I want to thank every member of the Meridian team for the contribution to this fundraise. In particular, the Investor Relations team led by Charlotte Vandenberg has done an exceptional job in coordinating an extraordinarily complex fundraise. The Investment teams across our European Industrials platform have engaged with prospective LPs throughout the year and have been instrumental in conveying the depth and quality of our work.

Fund V will be deployed across European industrial sectors with a particular focus on advanced manufacturing, energy transition, and specialty chemicals. We anticipate making 10 to 12 platform investments over the four-year deployment period, with typical equity tickets in the EUR 300 to 700 million range.

The fund close marks an important moment for the firm but also a moment of substantial responsibility. The LPs have placed exceptional trust in us. Our task is to deploy this capital with the same discipline and excellence that have characterised the Meridian platform from inception.

We will host an all-firm celebration on 18 December at the Zurich office. Details will follow separately.

Thank you all for everything you do.

Michael Vossberg
Chief Executive Officer
Meridian Capital
