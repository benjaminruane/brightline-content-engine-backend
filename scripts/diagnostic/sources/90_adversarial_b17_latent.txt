INVESTMENT MEMORANDUM — STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL

To:        Global Investment Committee
From:      European Industrials Team
Date:      15 March 2023
Subject:   Helios Grid Controls — post-close note

We invested last year in Helios Grid Controls GmbH (the "Company"), a Munich-headquartered supplier of grid-stabilisation software to European transmission operators. The investment was EUR 85 million of equity for a 62% controlling stake.

The Company generated revenue of EUR 41 million in the year of our investment. Headcount at close was 190 employees, based in Munich.
