INVESTMENT MEMORANDUM — STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL

To:        Investment Committee
From:      Healthcare Team — Anya Petrov (Member of Management), Dr. James Whitfield (Senior Advisor), Felix Mertens (Senior Vice President)
Date:      28 October 2025
Subject:   Project Caraway — Proposed acquisition of Bloom Nutrition Holdings Limited

RECOMMENDATION

We seek IC approval for an investment of EUR 195 million for a 72% controlling stake in Bloom Nutrition Holdings Limited ("Bloom" or "the Company"), a London-headquartered premium women's nutrition and wellness brand with a multi-channel European presence. The transaction values Bloom at an enterprise value of EUR 310 million, representing 14.8x trailing twelve months EBITDA of EUR 21 million.

The multiple appears full but reflects Bloom's growth profile (47% revenue CAGR over the past three years), premium positioning, and the strategic optionality created by a well-defined and proven brand platform.

COMPANY OVERVIEW

Bloom was founded in 2018 by Sarah Whitmore and Dr. Lara Bennett, both Cambridge-trained scientists. The Company operates in the premium women's health and nutrition category, offering a focused range of evidence-based supplement products targeting four primary use cases: hormonal balance, energy and recovery, skin and hair health, and pregnancy and postnatal nutrition.

The product range comprises 24 SKUs, all formulated in-house, manufactured under contract by GMP-certified European facilities, and sold direct-to-consumer through Bloom's own website and Amazon, alongside a select wholesale presence in premium retailers including Selfridges, Le Bon Marché, KaDeWe, and Sephora.

Bloom operates with a small but capable team of 67 people, headquartered in London with a 12-person team in Berlin focused on the DACH market. The Company has a clinical advisory board of seven specialists across endocrinology, nutrition, and women's health, which contributes to both product development and to the brand's clinical credibility.

FINANCIAL PROFILE

Trailing twelve months revenue is EUR 64 million, up from EUR 19 million in 2022 — representing a 47% compound annual growth rate. The recent twelve-month period has shown some deceleration to 31% year-on-year growth as the Company has scaled, but absolute growth remains strong and the unit economics have continued to improve.

Channel mix is approximately 64% direct-to-consumer (own website), 19% Amazon, 14% premium wholesale, and 3% from a small number of clinic-channel relationships in the UK and Germany. Geographic mix is approximately 48% UK, 28% DACH, 14% France, and 10% other (primarily Italy, Spain, and the Nordics).

Gross margin is 71.4%, reflecting the premium pricing and direct-to-consumer channel weight. EBITDA margin is 32.8%, which is exceptional for a brand at this growth stage and reflects both strong gross margins and disciplined marketing efficiency. Customer acquisition cost payback is 5.2 months on first-purchase margin, and 87% of revenue comes from repeat customers.

INVESTMENT THESIS

Three pillars support our investment thesis.

Premium women's health is a structurally attractive emerging category. Women's health has been historically under-served by both mainstream and premium consumer health brands. The category is growing at approximately 14% annually in Europe, with significantly faster growth in dedicated direct-to-consumer brands. Bloom is well-positioned within this category as a clinically-credible, premium-positioned, multi-product brand.

Bloom has built a defensible position in a category where most competitors are either single-product brands (limiting customer lifetime value) or insufficiently differentiated. The Company's clinical advisory board, in-house formulation capability, and disciplined product development cadence (typically two new product launches per year) create meaningful competitive moats.

International expansion runway is significant. Bloom is strong in the UK and increasingly strong in DACH, but underdeveloped in France, Italy, and Spain. The brand has demonstrable cross-border appeal — DACH revenue has grown from zero in 2021 to EUR 18 million in 2024 — and we see clear opportunity to replicate this in the Romance language markets.

RISKS

Customer acquisition cost inflation in direct-to-consumer is the principal risk. Bloom's current 5.2-month payback period has lengthened from 3.8 months in 2022, reflecting both increased competition and broader paid social media cost inflation. We have modelled continued payback elongation to 7 months by exit; the unit economics remain attractive at this level but the trajectory warrants attention.

Brand-driven categories are subject to founder-dependency risk. Sarah Whitmore in particular is closely associated with the brand through content, podcast appearances, and visible founder presence. Sarah has indicated commitment to remain CEO for at least four years post-investment but has expressed interest in stepping back over a longer horizon. We have spent time understanding the brand architecture and believe Bloom has built sufficient institutional brand strength to be sustainable beyond a single founder.

Regulatory risk in women's health is real. Several of Bloom's products make claims around hormonal support and pregnancy wellness that are subject to evolving regulatory scrutiny across European markets. The Company has invested appropriately in regulatory affairs and has a clean track record, but the regulatory environment for nutrition claims is becoming more demanding.

VALUE CREATION PLAN

We underwrite revenue growth from EUR 64 million in 2025 to approximately EUR 195 million by 2030, an 25% CAGR. The growth is sourced from continued UK growth (modest, reflecting maturation), continued strong DACH growth, accelerated entry into France and Spain, and selective US market entry in year three.

EBITDA margin is expected to expand modestly from 32.8% to 35% as scale benefits offset increased investment in international expansion and a measured US market entry.

The base case generates a 2.9x MOIC and 24% IRR over a five-year hold.

MANAGEMENT

Sarah Whitmore (CEO) and Dr. Lara Bennett (CSO) remain the principal value driving figures and both will rollover meaningful equity. The wider team is capable but relatively junior; we will recruit a Chief Marketing Officer and a Chief International Officer over the first 18 months.

TRANSACTION STRUCTURE

The transaction will be funded with EUR 195 million of Halden Group equity and EUR 75 million of senior debt. The founders will retain approximately 22% in aggregate; existing seed and Series A investors (Felix Capital, JamJar, Khosla Ventures) will be exited in full. Total leverage at close is 3.6x EBITDA.

CONCLUSION

Bloom is a high-quality premium consumer brand in a structurally growing women's health category with strong unit economics and a clear international expansion runway. The entry multiple is full but justified. We recommend approval.
