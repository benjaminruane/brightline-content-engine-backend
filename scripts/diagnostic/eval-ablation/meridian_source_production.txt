Meridian Capital Partners V, L.P. — Fund Summary (for prospective limited partners)

Fund: Meridian Capital Partners V, L.P. ("Fund V" or "the Fund")
Manager: Meridian Capital Management (the "GP")
Strategy: Lower-mid-market buyouts in European industrial technology and business services
Target fund size: EUR 1.2 billion (hard cap EUR 1.5 billion)
GP commitment: 3% of total commitments, funded in cash
Term: 10 years from final close, with two one-year extension options at GP discretion
Investment period: 5 years from final close
Management fee: 1.75% on committed capital during the investment period; 1.5% on invested capital thereafter
Carried interest: 20%, subject to an 8% preferred return and a 100% GP catch-up
First close: expected Q3 2026; final close targeted within 12 months of first close

Track record: Across Funds I–IV, Meridian has deployed EUR 2.8 billion across 41 platform investments. Realised gross MOIC of 2.4x and gross IRR of 21% on fully realised deals (17 exits). Fund IV (2019 vintage, EUR 900 million) is currently marked at 1.9x gross MOIC and 24% gross IRR, with 4 of 12 platform investments fully realised.

Team: 14 investment professionals across offices in London, Munich, and Stockholm. Founding partners Anna Lindqvist and Thomas Reiner have worked together since 2008. No senior departures across the last three fund cycles.

Sector focus: The Fund targets companies with EUR 20–100 million enterprise value, EUR 5–25 million EBITDA, and defensible positions in niche industrial or B2B services markets. Typical hold period is 4–6 years. The Fund expects to make 10–14 platform investments, with reserved capital for bolt-on acquisitions.

Diversification limits: No single investment to exceed 15% of total commitments. No more than 30% of commitments deployed outside the EU. No more than 20% in any single sub-sector.

ESG: The Fund is an Article 8 fund under SFDR and integrates ESG diligence into every investment decision. Annual ESG reporting provided to LPs.

Key risks disclosed: concentration in European industrials; currency exposure for non-EUR LPs; illiquidity over the fund term; reliance on continued availability of leverage in the lower-mid-market.
