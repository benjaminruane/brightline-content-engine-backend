Halden Group — Investment Committee note
Meridian Capital Partners V, L.P.
Prepared for IC discussion, Halden Group Private Markets
Confidential — internal use only

1. Recommendation

Halden Group recommends a lead commitment to Meridian Capital Partners V. The
commitment is sized within our existing European lower-mid-market allocation and
is consistent with the pacing plan agreed at the February 2025 strategy review.

We recommend the commitment on the balance of diligence completed to date. On
balance, we believe the fund should deliver returns broadly in line with its
predecessor.

2. Relationship history

Halden Group has invested with Meridian Capital on three co-investments and two
debt financings, and holds secondary LP positions in Fund III and Fund IV. The
working relationship with the Meridian partners has been constructive across
those transactions. Halden Group expects the relationship to deepen over the
life of the fund as Meridian builds out Fund V and as further co-investment
and secondaries opportunities arise.

Access to co-investment has been a material part of the relationship. The GP
has provided Halden with co-investment invitations on several prior deals that
would not otherwise have been available to us through the primary fund alone.

3. Diligence summary

Fund V targets lower-mid-market buyouts in European industrial technology and
business services at a target fund size of EUR 1.2 billion. The strategy,
hold period guidance of four to six years, and the EU diversification limit
(no more than 30 per cent of commitments outside the EU) are consistent with
the materials Meridian circulated to prospective LPs.

Track record diligence focused on realised performance across Funds I to IV.
Meridian has realised a gross MOIC of 2.4 times across 17 exits. We were
attracted to Meridian on the strength of that track record, which is, in our
view, genuinely exceptional relative to the peer set we reviewed for this
vintage. Diligence also noted that Fund IV is currently marked at 1.9 times
gross MOIC and a 24 per cent gross IRR, with four of twelve platform
investments fully realised.

Team diligence confirmed no senior departures across the last three fund
cycles. The founding partners remain in place. The team's stability is a
positive factor in our assessment of key-person risk.

4. Timing and process

Halden Group made a lead commitment to Meridian Capital Partners V in June
2025, ahead of the GP's planned first close. The commitment was approved under
the delegated authority of the Private Markets Investment Committee following
the May 2025 preliminary discussion.

5. Risks

Principal risks recorded for IC: concentration in European industrials;
currency exposure for non-EUR commitments; illiquidity over the fund term;
and reliance on continued availability of leverage in the lower mid-market.
None of these risks is judged to outweigh the recommendation above.

6. Closing

Halden Group expects the relationship to deepen over the life of the fund and
recommends the commitment.
