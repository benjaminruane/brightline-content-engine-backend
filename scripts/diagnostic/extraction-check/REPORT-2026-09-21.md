**Three-source projection.** The three slowest on-genre documents that succeeded: `3i-audited-fs-2025.pdf` 464,305 + `pg-ir-july-2025.pdf` 216,389 + `3i-press-release-fy2025.pdf` 205,120 = **885,814 ms**. The three slowest that would reach the function: 464,305 + 205,120 + 160,964 = **830,389 ms (277% of 300 s)**.

**The residual.** 300,000 minus 885,814 is **-585,814 ms**. There is no leftover for Review. B277 measured Review wall clocks with sources already extracted: 150 words 17 s, 500 words 20 s, 1,500 words 36 s. A 1,500-word Review with three real sources of this class **does not fit**. Extraction alone overruns the Function. The 36-second Review never starts.

**PRODUCT-BLOCKING** against D6. Every successful extract is over 10 seconds. Any three-source pack of the slow documents is over 20 seconds. The slowest single file overruns the 300-second Function by itself.

## Section 7. What is still broken

Only what this measurement shows.

- Time. 0 of 10 successful extracts finish in under 10 seconds. The slowest is 464 seconds, past the Function cap.
- The 60-to-300 timeout raise buys a returned string, not a Review. Four former timeouts now "succeed" too slowly to leave budget for the model.
- Chunks convert costs as much as the text convert and is not used by the matcher.
- Sources are sequential. Three real documents of this class cannot share one 300-second request with a Review.
- Upload guard still blocks four of eleven. MAX_PDF_MB still blocks the full annual report.
- Real source length is not 3,500 words. Median here is 6,199. Two files exceed 24,000.
