KELVEDGE GROUP
Investment Committee memorandum
STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL

Subject:  Fennovar - follow-on investment
Date:     4 February 2026
From:     Mid-Market Industrials team

1. Recommendation

We seek the Committee's approval for a follow-on investment of up to £95 million in
Fennovar, alongside existing shareholders, to fund the acquisition of a Spanish
competitor. Terms of that acquisition are not yet agreed.

We do not recommend any change to the carrying value at this time.

2. Background

Kelvedge invested £142 million in Fennovar in November 2024, as part of the
£318 million deployed into new investments in the year to 31 March 2025.

Fennovar is a European provider of specialist industrial coatings, headquartered in
Lyon, with manufacturing in France, Germany and Poland. The company sells primarily
to industrial original equipment manufacturers, with a smaller aftermarket business.

3. Trading

Revenue for the year to 31 December 2025 was €287 million, 14% ahead of the prior
year. EBITDA was €41 million, an increase of 18%, representing a margin of 14.3%.

The 18% EBITDA increase reflects price realisation, the full-year effect of the
Polish acquisition completed in 2024, and lower resin input costs.

The company holds an estimated 11% share of the European specialist coatings market
and is the second largest independent participant in France.

4. Investment case

Management expect revenue to reach €340 million by 2027, although this depends on the
successful integration of the two bolt-on acquisitions completed in the second half of
2025.

We see a credible path to an EBITDA margin of 16% over the hold period, contributing
approximately 180 basis points of the value we underwrote at entry.

The Spanish target would add roughly €60 million of revenue and would give Fennovar a
manufacturing presence in Iberia for the first time.

5. Risks

Raw material cost inflation remains the principal exposure. Customer concentration is
moderate: the largest customer represents 9% of revenue and the top ten represent 34%.
Integration risk on the two completed bolt-ons is not yet fully retired.

6. Next steps

If the Spanish transaction does not complete on acceptable terms, the team will either
return to the Committee with a revised proposal or recommend that the allocated
capital be released. We are not recommending any specific alternative transaction at
this stage.
