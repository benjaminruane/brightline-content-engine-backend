Kelvedge Group plc
Half-year results for the six months to 30 September 2025
13 November 2025

Steady progress in a slower market

- Total return of £1,724 million, or 7% on opening shareholders' funds (six months to
  30 September 2024: £2,109 million, 10%). Net asset value per share of 2,681 pence
  (31 March 2025: 2,542 pence).

- Our Private Equity business generated a gross investment return of £1,904 million,
  or 9% (six months to 30 September 2024: £2,215 million, 11%). The result was driven
  by Grulden, by continued margin progress at Skaldwick, and by a first full period of
  contribution from the three investments completed in the prior year.

- Grulden delivered like-for-like sales growth of 5.4% in the first eight periods of
  2025 (first eight periods of 2024: 9.1%). Net sales were €9,873 million (first eight
  periods of 2024: €8,614 million). Operating EBITDA was €1,204 million. 168 new
  stores had been added year to date and the cash balance at 30 September 2025 was
  €512 million.

- We continue to expect Grulden to open between 350 and 400 new stores in the 2025
  calendar year.

- Following completion of the audit, the Group's gross investment return for the year
  to 31 March 2025 has been restated from £5,211 million to £5,186 million, reflecting
  a revised valuation of one infrastructure holding. Total return for that year is
  unchanged at £5,049 million.

- Our Infrastructure business generated a gross investment return of £71 million, or
  4%. Income remained the principal contributor.

- The Private Equity team invested £486 million in the half year and generated
  £312 million of realised proceeds. No new platform investments were completed.

- Interim dividend of 30.5 pence per share, unchanged on the prior year interim.

- The Board will consider, alongside the full-year results, whether to recommend a
  special distribution or to retain the proceeds of the year's realisations for
  reinvestment. No decision has been taken.

Martin Ashcombe, Kelvedge's Chief Executive, commented:

"Trading across the portfolio has been steady in a market that is clearly slower than
the one we saw a year ago. Grulden continues to take share, though at a more measured
rate, and we are comfortable with the pace of store openings. We have been patient on
new investment, and we would rather remain so than pay prices we do not believe in."
