Cravenford Private Investments Fund ("CPIF")
Quarterly investor letter
First quarter 2026

20 April 2026

Dear Shareholder,

Performance

Class I shares returned -2.27% in the first quarter of 2026. Class A shares returned
-2.45%. Since the Fund's inception in April 2025, Class I has returned 3.54% and
Class A 3.35%, both on an annualised basis.

The decline in the quarter reflects a mark-down at two software holdings, the
strengthening of the dollar against the euro, and the timing of a single continuation
vehicle valuation that will now fall into the second quarter.

Net asset value

Following completion of the March valuation cycle, the Fund's net asset value at
31 March 2026 has been finalised at $661.4 million. The March factsheet, which was
published before the cycle closed, showed $653.0 million. The difference relates
entirely to the continuation vehicle referred to above.

The Fund held 57 companies at the quarter end, across more than 40 general partner
relationships.

Portfolio

North America represents 70% of the portfolio and Europe 25%, with small positions in
Asia and Australia. Direct investments represent 88% of the portfolio and secondaries
the balance.

Marnholt Networks was among the stronger contributors in the quarter. Piquesta Tire
was held flat. The two software holdings referred to above are not named here and will
be discussed at the annual meeting.

Fees

Management fees remain waived until 31 December 2026. Total annual expenses for
Class I after the waiver are 1.69%, against 4.35% before it. The equivalent figures
for Class A are 2.44% and 5.10%.

Outlook

We expect the direct portfolio to season through 2026, although the pace will depend
on exit conditions that have not yet improved. We are not forecasting a particular
level of realisations.

If exit conditions remain closed through the second half, the Board will either
maintain the current repurchase level or reduce it in order to preserve liquidity.
No decision has been taken and shareholders will be notified before any change.

We remain of the view that the seeded portfolio was acquired at attractive prices,
and that patience is the correct posture from here.

Cravenford Registered Advisers L.P.
