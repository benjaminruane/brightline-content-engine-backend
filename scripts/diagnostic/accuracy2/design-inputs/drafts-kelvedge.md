## K-D1  FY2025 results commentary
SOURCES: K1

1. Kelvedge Group delivered a total return of £5,049 million for the year to 31 March 2025, or 25% on opening shareholders' funds, against £3,839 million and 23% a year earlier.
2. Net asset value per share rose to 2,542 pence from 2,085 pence, after a 27 pence per share loss on foreign exchange translation.
3. The net total return of 25% was achieved against a challenging macro-economic and geopolitical backdrop.
4. The Private Equity business delivered a gross investment return of £5,113 million, or 26%, up from £4,059 million and 25%.
5. That return was driven by Grulden.
6. Skaldwick generated a gross investment return of £4,551 million, or 32% on its opening value.
7. Grulden delivered annual revenue growth of 22%, like-for-like sales growth of 10.3% and EBITDA growth of 29% in 2024.
8. The business started 2025 well, with net sales of EUR 3,521 million against EUR 3,004 million in the first three periods of 2024.
9. Operating EBITDA over the same periods was EUR 464 million, against EUR 397 million.
10. Run-rate EBITDA on a last-twelve-months basis to the third period of 2025 reached EUR 2,328 million, a 26% increase.
11. Private Equity contributed £5,113 million of the Group's £5,211 million gross investment return.
12. At the end of week 19, Grulden's cash balance stood at EUR 427 billion.
13. Grulden had added 76 new stores by mid-May.
14. The Private Equity team invested £1,177 million in the year, including £768 million in a further stake in Grulden.
15. Realised proceeds of £659 million came from the exits of Vyrenza and RD, both at money multiples of 2.0x or higher.
16. Infrastructure generated a gross investment return of £52 million, or 3%, against £99 million and 7% a year earlier.
17. Infrastructure returns fell because of higher interest rates.
18. The Group took £2.4 billion of cash proceeds out of the portfolio and ended the year with liquidity of £1,323 million and gearing of 3%.

## K-D4  Fennovar investment note
SOURCES: K1, K4

1. Kelvedge invested £318 million in Fennovar in the year to 31 March 2025.
2. Fennovar is a European provider of specialist industrial coatings, headquartered in Lyon.
3. The company manufactures in France, Germany and Poland.
4. Revenue for the year to 31 December 2025 was EUR 287 million, 14% ahead of the prior year.
5. EBITDA was EUR 41 million, an increase of 18%, representing a margin of 14.3%.
6. The EBITDA increase reflects price realisation and lower resin input costs.
7. Fennovar is the dominant independent participant in the European specialist coatings market.
8. Management expect revenue to reach EUR 340 million by 2027.
9. That expectation depends on the successful integration of the two bolt-on acquisitions completed in the second half of 2025.
10. The team sees a credible path to an EBITDA margin of 16% over the hold period.
11. The largest customer represents 9% of revenue and the top ten represent 34%.
12. Raw material cost inflation remains the principal exposure.
13. The team is seeking approval for a follow-on investment of up to £95 million.
14. The follow-on would fund the acquisition of a Spanish competitor, on terms that are not yet agreed.
15. The Spanish target would add roughly EUR 60 million of revenue.
16. It would give Fennovar a manufacturing presence in Iberia for the first time.
17. The team recommends no change to the carrying value at this time.
18. Integration risk on the two completed bolt-ons has not been fully retired.
19. Fennovar was one of three new investments completed that year, alongside Cloverbeck and Wenbach Pruefservice.
20. The company sells primarily to industrial original equipment manufacturers.
