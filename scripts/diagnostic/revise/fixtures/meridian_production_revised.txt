In June 2026, Partners Group committed to Meridian Capital Partners V, a EUR 1.2 billion flagship fund from Meridian Capital targeting lower-mid-market buyouts in European industrial technology and business services companies. The fund intends to build a portfolio of 10-14 control-oriented investments, with equity checks of EUR 80-100 million apiece. Target companies are expected to have enterprise valuations in the range of EUR 20-100 million, annual EBITDA of EUR 5-25 million and defensible positions in niche industrial or B2B markets. Partners Group was attracted to this investment given Meridian Capital's strong track record on its prior vintage funds, coupled with its well-established investment team and operational approach to value creation. Furthermore, Partners Group has a long-established relationship with the manager, having invested alongside Meridian Capital on several co-investments and debt financings, while also making several secondary investments in its earlier funds.
