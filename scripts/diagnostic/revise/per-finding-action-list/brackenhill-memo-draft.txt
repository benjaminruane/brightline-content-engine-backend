Brackenhill Capital Partners III: Investment Recommendation

Brackenhill Capital Partners III closed at $840 million in March 2024, above its
$750 million target.

The fund has delivered a net IRR of 18.4% since inception.

The fund is currently marked at 1.9 times gross MOIC.

Three full exits have been completed from Fund II at a blended 2.1 times gross
MOIC.

Fund II's realisations were, in our view, an exceptional outcome for a
first-time institutional vehicle.

The firm's senior team has been stable, which means execution risk on Fund III
is limited.

We have known the partners since 2019, and in our experience they are candid
about underperformance.

Comparable managers in North American healthcare services have returned a median
2.3 times gross MOIC over the same period.

Brackenhill expects to hold a first close on Fund IV in the third quarter of
2026.

On balance, we are supportive of the commitment.
