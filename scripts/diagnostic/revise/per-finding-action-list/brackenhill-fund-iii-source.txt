BRACKENHILL CAPITAL PARTNERS
Fund III Investor Update
Quarter ended 30 June 2026

FUND OVERVIEW

Brackenhill Capital Partners III (the "Fund") held its final close in March 2024
at $840 million in aggregate commitments, ahead of its $750 million target. The
Fund invests in lower-mid-market healthcare services businesses in North
America, with a focus on multi-site providers and outsourced clinical support.

PORTFOLIO AND PERFORMANCE

As at 30 June 2026 the Fund has completed six platform investments and two
bolt-on acquisitions. Approximately 71% of committed capital has been deployed
or reserved for follow-on investment.

The portfolio is currently marked at 1.4 times gross MOIC. Net internal rate of
return since inception is 11.2%. No portfolio company has been fully realised
from Fund III to date, and these figures therefore reflect unrealised valuations
prepared in accordance with the Fund's valuation policy.

PREDECESSOR FUND

Brackenhill Capital Partners II is fully invested. Three full exits have been
completed from Fund II, at a blended 2.1 times gross MOIC. Two further portfolio
companies remain held.

TEAM

The investment team comprises fourteen professionals across offices in Chicago
and Nashville. Of the four founding partners, two remain with the firm. One
founding partner departed in 2025 to pursue interests outside private equity,
and one moved to a non-executive role in 2023.

The firm appointed a Head of Value Creation in January 2026, bringing operating
experience from two multi-site provider platforms.

FORWARD PLANS

The firm expects to hold a first close on Brackenhill Capital Partners IV in the
first quarter of 2027. No formal marketing has commenced.

VALUATION POLICY

Portfolio company valuations are prepared quarterly by the investment team,
reviewed by the firm's valuation committee, and subject to annual review by an
independent third party. Valuations reflect market comparables and recent
transaction multiples as at the reporting date.
