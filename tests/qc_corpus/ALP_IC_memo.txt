Investment Committee Memorandum — Project Adriatic

Recommendation: We recommend an investment of EUR 145 million for a 55% controlling stake in Adriatic Logistics Partners (the Company), a mid-market operator of warehousing and distribution assets across the Adriatic region.

Business overview: The Company operates six distribution centres located in Croatia, Slovenia, and northern Italy. Its network serves regional retail and industrial clients under multi-year service agreements.

Financial performance: The Company generated revenue of EUR 92 million in FY2024, up from EUR 71 million in FY2023, representing sustained double-digit growth over the period. Reported EBITDA margin expanded to 24% in FY2024, from 19% two years prior, driven by network densification and improved asset utilisation.

Workforce: The Company employs 210 staff across its six distribution centres, with the majority based at the Zagreb headquarters.

Advisers: The transaction was advised by Elena Foscari, former operations director at Veneto Freight, who supported the commercial diligence workstream.

Returns: Our base case generates a 2.4x MOIC and a 19% gross IRR over a five-year hold.
