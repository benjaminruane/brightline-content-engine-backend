Update to Investment Committee — Project Adriatic (Post-Diligence)

This memo updates the Committee following completion of confirmatory diligence on Adriatic Logistics Partners.

Workforce: The Company employs 240 staff as of March 2025, not 210 as stated in our initial memo, reflecting two distribution-centre openings completed during the diligence period.

Commercial pipeline: Management described the forward pipeline as “robust and well-diversified”, supported by a weighted-average contract tenor of just over four years.

Returns: Our updated base case generates a 2.3x MOIC and an 18% gross IRR, marginally below the initial underwrite.
